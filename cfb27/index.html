How this works

Every call is generated from the matchup, not from a fixed list. The generator reads your playbook's style (formation pool, tempo, run/pass identity) against the opponent playbook's DNA — box count, coverage shell, man/zone split, pressure rate, press tendency — and picks the concept whose strengths attack what that defense gives up, then dresses it with a formation from your book, a motion and a protection.

All 138 CFB 27 team offensive playbooks, all 11 scheme playbooks (Air Raid, Go Go, Multiple, Option, Pistol, Power Spread, Pro Style, Run & Shoot, Spread, Spread Option, Veer & Shoot) and all 31 defensive playbooks are included. Formation names are CFB 27's own — checked against the game's formation database, with Madden-only sets (Gun Empty Trey, Gun Doubles Off, Jumbo, Swinging Gate) deliberately left out. Each team's style tag reflects that program's real offensive identity — swap to a scheme playbook any time you disagree with a tag. Works offline once loaded, so add it to your home screen and run it on the couch.
